## Reflection

**Zaw Phyo Aung · 6631503130 · Product Owner · Team FLUX**

### 1. Lessons learned: building with AI

As Product Owner I connected the problem, the user research and the backlog. **What got better:** AI made changing requirements cheaper. When we decided that Students should choose an Advisor for each booking, the team could update the database, interface, tests and documents in one change instead of over weeks. AI also helped us audit the documents, so features were clearly labelled as implemented, deployment-dependent, planned, or waiting for policy approval.

**What got worse:** AI made it tempting to add scope and to write claims that sounded stronger than our evidence. Our five student interviews support visible remote use, reliable availability and conflict prevention. They do **not** validate the Technician → Advisor → Dean approval workflow, which comes from institutional assumptions. I had to keep that boundary clear. We also never ran timed real-user sessions, so speed and error rate stay "Not measured". AI cannot replace talking to users, and it cannot decide what the product should be.

### 2. Where I stand in the AI era

When AI can build features quickly, the hard question becomes *what* to build and *why*. My strength is turning user evidence into priorities and keeping product claims honest. I need more practice in measuring outcomes with real users.

### 3. My plan

**Next year:** improve my skills in user research, product analytics and agile delivery, and get an internship as a business analyst or junior product owner. **In five years:** work as a product manager who leads AI-assisted products, grounded in real user evidence and responsible use of data.
