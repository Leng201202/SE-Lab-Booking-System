# FLUX Company Charter

## Company identity

**Company name:** FLUX  
**Team size:** 5 members  
**Project:** SE Lab PC Booking System

FLUX works as one cross-functional software team. Each member has a primary area of ownership, while the whole team remains responsible for delivering a usable, secure, tested, and honestly documented product.

## Team rules

1. **One accountable owner:** every backlog item, design decision, test, and presentation section has one named owner, while other members may contribute.
2. **Evidence before claims:** a feature is considered complete only when implementation evidence and appropriate verification exist. Test results and presentation numbers must have reproducible sources.
3. **Review before merge:** important code, database, security, and design changes must be reviewed by the relevant role owner. A member must not approve their own high-risk work alone.
4. **Raise blockers early:** blockers, failed tests, security concerns, and schedule risks must be communicated as soon as they are discovered. Open issues are recorded rather than hidden.
5. **Protect the agreed scope:** the Product Owner prioritizes scope changes after the team discusses their technical, design, testing, and deadline impact.
6. **Use AI responsibly:** AI output is treated as a draft, not as proof. A named team member must review, test, and own it. Secrets and personal data must not be shared with AI tools, and material AI use must be disclosed.
7. **Document important decisions:** decisions affecting requirements, architecture, design, testing, or release readiness must be recorded in the repository so everyone works from the same information.
8. **Use a shared definition of done:** work is complete only when it meets its acceptance criteria, preserves privacy and role boundaries, passes the available checks, and includes updated documentation.
9. **Resolve disagreements constructively:** the relevant role owner leads an evidence-based discussion. Unresolved scope decisions go to the Product Owner, while technical or release-safety concerns must be documented before a final decision.
10. **Share presentation responsibility:** every member must explain the work they personally built, designed, governed, or tested and be prepared to answer questions about their role.

## Working agreement

- Team members communicate respectfully and give progress updates before agreed deadlines.
- Work is assigned according to role ownership, capacity, and project priority.
- Changes that affect another member's area are discussed with that member before completion.
- Quality, security, accessibility, and privacy concerns may be raised by any team member.
- The team reports incomplete work and limitations honestly instead of presenting assumptions as finished results.

## Charter agreement

By following this charter, FLUX agrees to maintain clear ownership, responsible collaboration, evidence-based delivery, and shared accountability throughout the project.
