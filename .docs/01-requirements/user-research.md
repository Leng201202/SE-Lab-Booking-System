# User research — interview guide and findings

This file preserves the recorded research evidence. Do not fabricate participants, quotes, consent, or findings. The project team must retain the original notes and confirm that these five entries represent real interviews.

## Discussion guide

1. Walk me through the last time you needed a lab computer.
2. Has a computer you were using or planning to use been taken by someone else?
3. Do you use lab computers remotely? How do others know the machine is in use?
4. How do you currently know whether a PC is operational and free?
5. What information would make you trust an availability calendar?
6. How should a booking request and its approval status be communicated?
7. What would make the system unsafe, confusing, or too inconvenient to use?

## Findings summary

Five records identify two recurring themes:

1. Remote computer use is not physically visible, so a machine may appear free.
2. Students need reliable availability and reservation conflict prevention.

These findings support the availability, access-mode, booking, and overlap requirements. They do not independently validate the Advisor/Dean workflow, trusted-role administration, legal policy, notifications, recurring bookings, or operational rules; those require institutional stakeholder evidence.
